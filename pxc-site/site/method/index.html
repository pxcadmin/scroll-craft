The PXC Method

Pinpoint. eXecute. Compound.

Find the friction. Do the work. Keep it working. Three steps, in that order, over 90 days.

How does the PXC Method work?

The PXC Method finds the three front-desk problems driving the rest, fixes them with your own team, and builds the routines that keep them fixed. Each step has a plain output you can hold: a count, a rule, a number.

Pinpoint.

Find what starts the chain reaction.

One missed handoff rarely stays one problem. Pinpoint is how we find the one that started it. Your team runs a five-day count alongside the normal day. We observe your hardest hour. Then you choose and sign a Top 3.

  • A five-day count
  • An observation of your hardest hour
  • A Top 3 you sign

Days 1 to 6 of the Reset

eXecute.

Put the fixes into everyday use.

A fix that lives in a binder is not a fix. Each of your three priorities gets a rule your team can read in a minute, one named owner, a script, hands-on training, and a number tracked every week.

  • A written rule
  • A named owner
  • Scripts and hands-on training
  • A number your team tracks each week

Days 7 to 80 of the Reset

Compound.

Make the better day repeatable.

The work holds when the routine holds. Three rhythms keep it going after we leave: a daily huddle, a weekly scoreboard, and a monthly owner review. They are short, and they are yours.

  • A daily huddle
  • A weekly scoreboard
  • A monthly owner review
