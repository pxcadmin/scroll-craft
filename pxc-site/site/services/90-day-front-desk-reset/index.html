Questions about the Reset.

How much staff time does the Reset take?

Plan on a few hours a week from the owner and the manager. The front desk team tallies the five-day count alongside the normal day, then practices each new routine in short sessions. The work is built to fit inside the day your team already has, not on top of it.

What if a front desk person leaves mid-Reset?

The Reset is built for that. Every fix has a written rule, a named owner and a script, so the routine belongs to the practice and not to one person. A new hire is trained from the same pages, and the weekly number keeps counting.

Do you replace staff with AI?

No. AI is set up only where your audit shows it will help your team, such as after-hours call capture, reminders and confirmations, or message sorting. Your team is trained on it and we count whether it worked. If AI is not the answer, we will say so.

What is outside the Reset?

EHR builds, billing projects and hiring searches are outside the Reset. So is clinical care. The Reset covers three front-desk priorities chosen by your audit and signed by you, and it stays on those three so they are finished and measured.

What if we have more than two locations?

The Reset is built for practices with one or two locations. The price covers one location, and each additional location is $3,000. Health systems are not a fit. If you have three or more sites, call (941) 239-2309 and we will tell you plainly whether it makes sense.

Can we start without the audit?

Yes. Every Reset begins with the same five-day count, so the Practice Audit is not a requirement. If you have already done the audit, your $1,500 is credited when you sign within 30 days of your findings call.
