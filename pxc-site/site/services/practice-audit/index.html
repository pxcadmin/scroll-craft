What is the Practice Audit?

The Practice Audit is a five-business-day count of your front desk, an observation of your hardest hour, and a findings call that ends with a Top 3 you sign. It costs $1,500 and it uses no patient information.

Most practices already know the front desk is under strain. What they do not have is a count. Without one, every fix is a guess, and the loudest problem wins. The audit replaces the guess with five days of tallies, so you can see which problems are driving the others and which ones can wait.

Count the break before you buy a tool for it.

How the five days run.

  1. Days 1 to 5Your team tallies calls, lines, overrides, no-shows and open messages alongside the normal day. Tick marks, not charts.
  2. Your hardest hourWe observe the hour your desk finds hardest, so the count has context.
  3. Findings callWe walk through your Domino Map and what the count shows.
  4. Your Top 3You choose and sign the three priorities worth fixing first.

What do you get?

Five things, written for your desk. They are yours to keep whether or not you go on to the Reset.

  1. The five-day count, on one sheet
  2. An observation of your hardest hour
  3. Your Domino Map: which of the ten dominoes are falling, and in what order
  4. A signed Top 3: the three priorities to fix first
  5. A quick-win kit your team can use right away

What it costs.

$1,500

Credited to the 90-Day Reset if you sign within 30 days of your findings call.

No patient information. Ever.

Your team counts events, not people. No names, no charts, no access to your EHR. The sheet holds tallies and nothing else.

What happens next?

If the count shows three problems worth 90 days of work, the 90-Day Reset fixes them with your team. If it does not, you will hear that plainly.
