About the audit

What do these terms mean?

Five-day count
Five business days of tallies kept by your own team: calls, lines, overrides, no-shows and open messages. No patient information.
Domino Map
A one-page picture of which of the ten front desk dominoes are falling at your practice, and in what order.
Top 3
The three priorities you choose and sign after the count. The Reset fixes these and only these.
Named owner
One person responsible for a workflow. Not a department, not "the front desk." A name.
Daily huddle
A short daily check-in where the team looks at today and yesterday's misses.
Weekly scoreboard
The few numbers your team tracks each week to see whether the fix is holding.
Monthly owner review
A monthly look at the scoreboard by the practice owner, so the routine outlasts the Reset.
Keep-Working Promise
If the day-90 measure is missed and the practice met its side of the plan, PXC keeps working at no added fee through day 180.
