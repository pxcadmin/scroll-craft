Our promise

Miss the number? We keep working.

If your day-90 measure is missed and your practice met its side of the plan, we keep working on the same three priorities at no added fee, through day 180.

What is the Keep-Working Promise?

It is what happens if the 90-Day Reset does not reach the number you signed. We do not walk away at day 90 with the work unfinished. We stay on the same three priorities, at no added fee, for up to 90 more days.

How does it work?

  • On day 6 you sign your three priorities and the measure for each one.
  • On day 90 we count again, on the same sheet we used on day one.
  • If a signed measure is missed and your practice met its side of the plan, the work continues on those priorities through day 180.

What is our side of the plan?

Your side is written into the plan you sign on day 6, in plain words, before the work starts. It is the practice's share of the work: the count gets tallied, each workflow has a named owner, the training sessions happen, and the team uses the new routine.

What the promise is not.

  • It is not a refund. The promise is more work, not money back.
  • It is not a guarantee of results. Results are measured and reported, not guaranteed.
  • It does not add new priorities. It covers the same three you signed.
  • It ends on day 180.

Why make it?

Because an operator stays until the work holds. If we agreed on a number and your team did its part, the remaining work is ours to finish.
