The map

Which problems knock the others over?

One missed handoff rarely stays one problem. We map your front desk as ten dominoes and fix the three that are knocking over the rest.

  1. 1

    Phones and Callbacks.

    A missed call with no owner is still open at the end of the day, and the patient calls again.

  2. 2

    Arrival and Check-In.

    Forms, insurance questions and a line at the window all start at the same minute.

  3. 3

    Wait Time and Flow.

    A delay nobody names feels longer, and the desk absorbs the frustration.

  4. 4

    Scheduling Integrity.

    Overrides with no rule fill the day past what the team can actually run.

  5. 5

    No-Shows and Confirmations.

    A reminder goes out, nobody owns the reply, and an open slot sits empty.

  6. 6

    Checkout and Follow-Through.

    The visit ends at the door, and the next step is nobody's task.

  7. 7

    Admin Requests and Messages.

    Refills, forms, records and portal notes share one pile and no turnaround.

  8. 8

    Handoffs and Escalation.

    Front-to-clinical handoffs depend on who happens to be nearby.

  9. 9

    Team Stability and Onboarding.

    A new hire inherits whatever was never written down, and the pattern repeats.

  10. 10

    Reputation and Feedback.

    Reviews are often the last domino. Nine others fell first.

The PXC Method

Find the friction. Do the work. Keep it working.

  1. Pinpoint.

    Find what starts the chain reaction. A five-day count, an observation of your hardest hour, and a Top 3 you sign.

  2. eXecute.

    Put the fixes into everyday use. A written rule. A named owner. Scripts and hands-on training. A number your team tracks each week.

  3. Compound.

    Make the better day repeatable. A daily huddle, a weekly scoreboard and a monthly owner review.
