Who is Jennifer Radu?

Jennifer Radu is a medical practice operator with more than 21 years in the work. She holds the C.P.P.M. credential, taught before she managed, and founded Patient eXperience Consulting in Bradenton, Florida, to fix one part of a practice well: the front desk.

The practice.

Jennifer has spent over 21 years running medical practices. At one point that meant 12 urgent care clinics across four counties, all at once. She knows what a Monday morning sounds like when three lines are ringing and one person is at the desk, because that was her desk.

The patient.

Six back surgeries put Jennifer on the other side of the window. She has been the patient on hold, the patient in the line, and the patient who called back to find out what happened to a message. That is why PXC measures the day from the front desk outward.

The person doing the work.

Before she ran practices, Jennifer taught. It shows in how a Reset runs. A new routine is not announced. It is written down, practiced with the people who will use it, and counted every week until it holds.

An operator, not a binder.

Jennifer brings all three to your team: change the work, teach the new routine, keep it going.
