# BRIEF: PXC

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. Source: the PXC Website Build Report (Nov 17, 2026), which fixes copy, order, palette, type and motion rules. No interview was run.

- **Vibe:** calm, warm, exact, operator-grade. A consultancy, not a startup.
- **Grammar:** chaptered document with two pinned scenes (hero, dominoes), as the guide requires. No third pin.
- **Feeling curve:** recognition (the busy desk) > relief (through the doorway) > grounded (numbers, short answer) > "that is us" (tabs, calculator) > the peak (ten dominoes fall, three stand back up) > trust (method, offer, promise) > proof (the blank sheet) > person (Jennifer) > resolve (the close).
- **Peak:** "I watched ten dominoes fall and tapped the three that are my front desk." Largest span after the hero; it hands the visitor straight into the screener with their three.
- **Signature move:** picked dominoes stand back up and glow, and the selection travels into the screener, which reports whether the count agreed.
- **Tell-someone sentence:** "It's the site where you walk through a doorway from a ringing front desk into a quiet one."
- **Assets:** supplied logos and portrait. No image generation was available, so the hero world is built in CSS 3D instead of photographic plates.
- **One action:** Take the free screener.
